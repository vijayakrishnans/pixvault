# Epic: pixvault-team-media (PV)

**ImgBed's speed and API, made for teams: shared libraries, your bucket or ours, and safe to host for strangers.
CloudFlare-ImgBed is a fast single-owner image host with one admin password and free "channels" (Telegram, Discord,
Hugging Face) as storage. Pixvault keeps its upload, serving, transform, tag, random-image, moderation and WebDAV
behaviour and its upload API. It rebuilds the rest for many teams on one deployment: Cirrus orgs and roles, libraries
(shared drives) with their own access, a strongly consistent per-library index in a Durable Object, R2 or the team's
own S3 bucket as the only storage, and abuse handling built in from the first upload.**

Pixvault is for teams that need somewhere to put images and files: marketing teams with brand assets, blogs with post
images, product teams with screenshots, and CI pipelines that publish artefacts. When it ships, a team can sign up,
invite teammates and guests with per-library roles, upload directly and resumably, serve public files fast and
private files by signed URL on its own domain, browse and organise together live, share by link, and keep using
PicGo, ShareX or the `satellite` client by changing a base URL and a token.

Built from [CloudFlare-ImgBed](https://github.com/MarSeventh/CloudFlare-ImgBed) and
[Sanyue-ImgHub](https://github.com/MarSeventh/Sanyue-ImgHub) (both MIT). Not affiliated with MarSeventh or Cloudflare.
